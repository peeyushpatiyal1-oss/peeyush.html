About The Championship

Welcome to Apex Arena, organized in partnership with ESL and regional gaming guilds. Compete for a prize pool of ₹5,00,000 across PC, mobile, and console platforms.

Watch daily live matches, tactical breakdowns, and behind-the-scenes player interviews.

"Victory is not about reflexes alone; it's about strategy, communication, and heart under pressure."

Tournament Gear & Rules

Official 240Hz gaming monitors and RTX-powered rigs will be provided.
All players must report to the venue 45 minutes before match call time.
Third-party crosshair overlays, macros, and scripts will result in an immediate permanent ban.

Top Tournament Titles

  • Valorant (5v5 Tactical Shooter)
  • Battlegrounds Mobile India (Squad Battle Royale)
  • Tekken 8 (1v1 Fighting Championship)
  • Counter-Strike 2 (5v5 Bomb Defusal)

Steps to Compete

  1. Form a verified 5-player squad (plus 1 optional substitute)
  2. Submit your team registration in the form below
  3. Join our official Discord server for lobby codes and referee checks
  4. Collect your physical player badge at the arena desk
